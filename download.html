Official download and publisher

This is the official website for Portal17 Studio. The application is developed and published by Portal17, the publisher shown on its Microsoft Store listing. The official product ID is 9MX9PN25FSGB.

Open Portal17 Studio on Microsoft Store ↗

The button opens the product listing on apps.microsoft.com. Microsoft Store handles the application download, installation and purchase. Review the product name and publisher before installing.

Price, free trial and purchase

Portal17 Studio is paid software. A free trial is available through Microsoft Store so you can evaluate the application before buying it. Trial availability, eligibility and duration are governed by the offer presented by Microsoft Store.

The current price, currency, taxes and any promotional discount are displayed on the Store listing and purchase screen for your region. Check those details and the trial conditions before starting a trial or purchasing. Prices and promotions may change.

Microsoft manages payment, purchase records and Store licensing. Review the Microsoft Store Terms of Sale and our terms and licensing information. Refund eligibility depends on the offer, Microsoft's terms and applicable law; see Microsoft's refund guidance.

Requirements and installation

Portal17 Studio runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11 PCs. Check the system requirements in the Store listing for your device. Store installation and licensing can require signing in to your Microsoft account.

  1. Open the official Microsoft Store listing linked above.
  2. Review the publisher, price or trial offer, system requirements and permissions.
  3. Choose the trial or purchase option you want and follow Microsoft's installation prompts.
  4. Open Portal17 Studio from the Windows Start menu. Record a task or build a visual flow, review its actions, then run it.

Portal17 Studio automates browser pages and Windows applications. Depending on the features you choose, it can read and write files, interact with desktop controls, connect to mailboxes, databases and APIs, and run schedules or triggers. The privacy policy explains recording, permissions, local storage and optional connections.

Stop automation and uninstall

  1. Stop running flows and recording. Disable enabled triggers, background execution and scheduled flows in Portal17 Studio.
  2. Close the app and any Portal17 Studio background service.
  3. Open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps (Apps & features on Windows 10).
  4. Find Portal17 Studio, choose Uninstall and follow the Windows prompts.

Uninstalling the app may leave projects, exported files, downloaded attachments, backups and user configuration on your computer. Review retention and deletion controls before removing data you want to keep. Microsoft account purchases and data already sent to connected services are managed through those services.

Privacy and support

Workflows run on your computer. Optional actions exchange data with the websites and services you configure. Read the privacy policy before connecting accounts or recording sensitive information.

For product, installation or licensing questions, contact contact@portal17studio.com. Publisher details and support information are on our support page.
