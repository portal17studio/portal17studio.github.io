Get product support

Email contact@portal17studio.com with your app version, Windows version, the action you were trying to perform and the error message. You can review the questions and answers, demos and installation and removal instructions.

Share only the information needed for the issue. Do not send passwords, access tokens, API keys or confidential email contents. Review and redact screenshots, reports and sample flows before attaching them. Our privacy policy explains how information sent to support is handled.

Store purchases and licensing

Microsoft Store handles payment, order records, installations and Store licences. For a transaction or refund question, consult the purchase terms and Microsoft support links. We can help with questions about using Portal17 Studio.

Report a privacy or security concern

Use the same contact address to report a privacy or security issue. Describe the affected feature and how to reproduce the issue using sample data. Avoid including working credentials or other people's private information.
